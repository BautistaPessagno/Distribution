# Understand the reference-product landscape

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

What do the supplied reference products actually do, and which verified mechanisms should inform an independent MarketingOS?

## Answer

The products are complementary references rather than interchangeable all-in-one systems. Marketing AGI supplies reasoning patterns, The Agentcy supplies a structured creative workspace, Maxfusion supplies production-workflow ideas that mostly exceed the MVP dependency boundary, and HumanPost supplies human distribution operations. See [the primary-source comparison](../research/reference-products.md).
